The Challenge & The Mission

Building Inclusive, Person-Centered Communities

The public policy landscape surrounding disability services is intricate. Finding the balance between high-quality support structures, Medicaid financing compliance, and long-term sustainability is a monumental task for any organization or government entity.


Potentia provides independent research, targeted systems-change strategies, and specialized training to public and private organizations. We empower you to navigate these complexities and drive transformative, sustainable results.

True reform begins with actionable intelligence.

Abuse Prevention & Ethics Certification Program

A train-the-trainer certification workshop that equips organizations serving people with intellectual and developmental disabilities to build lasting cultures of safety, ethics, and accountability.

Why It Matters

Prevention Is the Only Sustainable Strategy

Across the country, abuse and neglect in I/DD services reflect systemic weaknesses in prevention and reporting, not isolated incidents. A joint federal review found that group homes failed to report a majority of critical incidents to law enforcement or state agencies as required. A 2018 FiveThirtyEight investigation identified at least 2,400 allegations of abuse and neglect across more than 2,000 unlicensed California adult day programs, and licensed facilities have been repeatedly cited under Title 22 for personal-rights violations, supervision failures, and failure to report.


Once harm occurs, organizations face steep consequences: multi-million-dollar verdicts, insurance premium increases of 25% to 100% or more, reputational damage, and regulatory scrutiny. Prevention — not reaction — is the only strategy that protects the people served and stabilizes the organization.

Structured Training & Ethical Frameworks

Clear ethical standards, practical guidance for boundary-setting, and tools to recognize early warning signs of abuse or neglect, delivered as ongoing, scenario-based training rather than a one-time compliance exercise.

Supervision & Accountability

Guidance on staff-to-client ratios, monitoring high-risk situations such as transportation and personal care, and building regular performance and compliance reviews into daily operations.

Transparent Reporting Systems

Clear escalation pathways and a culture that encourages staff to report concerns without fear of retaliation, so warning signs surface before harm occurs.

The Certification

Abuse Prevention & Ethics Train-the-Trainer Certification Workshop

Sponsored by the Potentia Public Policy Institute, this certification workshop equips organizations with internal trainers who can sustain prevention efforts, embed ethical decision-making into daily practice, and strengthen organizational culture around safety and accountability. Participants train using Potentia's Preventing the Maltreatment of Vulnerable Individuals employee training booklet (California Edition), covering mandated reporting requirements, recognizing abuse, neglect, mistreatment, and financial abuse, and building program-level abuse prevention plans.
